## Delivered correction

Only `src/project/store.ts` and `test/storePersistenceLineage.test.ts` change executable behavior/contracts. Evidence is under this directory.

The store captures a private content lineage alongside the receipt's private target authority at save submission. Successful remote reload, reconnect, and `adoptProject` advance that lineage and clear the current receipt. Transaction rollback restores the original lineage with the original receipt. Receipt currentness checks the captured lineage in addition to generation, target, enabled/loaded state, and reference identity. A late save still returns its historical accepted receipt, but cannot publish over a replacement lineage's receipt (or restore a cleared one).

This deliberately does **not** advance `mutationGeneration` on load: its local-first dirty/catch-up semantics remain unchanged. No save response or proof read replaces live content. Ordinary replace/replaceProject return regions, apply adapter, session publication, acceptance/sticky behavior, and proof projection were not edited. No dependency, schema, framework, UI, or manual reload policy change.
