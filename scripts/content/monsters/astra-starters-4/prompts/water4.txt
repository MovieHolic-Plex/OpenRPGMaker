LINE: WATER starter line (stage 3 becomes water/ice). A baby walrus family. Design thread: a round white whisker muzzle with two tusks, and a water-spout fountain on its head that freezes.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby walrus pup, a huge round head with enormous round bright eyes, a round puffy white muzzle with two tiny rounded tusks, smooth sky-blue body with a white belly, two front flippers, one small water-spout fountain on top of its head.
STAGE 2: a chunky young walrus sitting up proudly, longer tusks, a taller water-spout crest, flippers with one wave-shaped edge.
STAGE 3 (water/ice): a huge strong walrus guardian rearing up, two long smooth ice-spear tusks, a white beard muzzle, one big frozen fountain crown of pale-cyan ice on its head, a few large ice plates on its shoulders.
Main colour sky blue with white; accent pale cyan ice / aqua only on the spout and ice.
