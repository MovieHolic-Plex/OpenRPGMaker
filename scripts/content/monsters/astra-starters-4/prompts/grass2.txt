LINE: GRASS starter line (stage 3 becomes grass/psychic). A koala family. Design thread: big round ears that are leaves, and a seed pod held to its chest that grows into a tree.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby koala, huge round head, enormous round bright eyes, a big dark oval nose, soft mint-green fur with a cream belly, two big round ears made of broad fuzzy leaves, hugging a big brown acorn-like seed pod with a tiny sprout to its chest, sitting.
STAGE 2: a sturdy young koala standing upright, the leaf ears grown larger and pointed, the seed pod opened into a small leafy sapling growing out of its back, strong climbing claws.
STAGE 3 (grass/psychic): a big calm forest-guardian koala sitting upright like a sage, a full round tree canopy of leaves growing from its back above its head with small glowing pink-violet blossoms, very large leaf ears, wise half-closed kind eyes.
Main colour mint green with cream; accent pink-violet only on the blossoms.
