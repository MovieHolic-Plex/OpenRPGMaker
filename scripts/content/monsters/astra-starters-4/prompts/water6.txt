LINE: WATER starter line (stage 3 becomes water/ice). A platypus family that stays a FOUR-LEGGED creature in every stage (never standing upright like a penguin). Design thread: a broad flat pale-yellow duck bill, a wide paddle tail, and a water bubble that becomes an ice crest.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby platypus on four stubby webbed feet, a huge round head with enormous round bright eyes and a big broad pale-yellow duck bill smiling, smooth sky-blue body with a white belly, a wide flat paddle tail with one big round water bubble on its tip.
STAGE 2: a bigger sleek platypus on four strong webbed legs, a longer bill, a single curling wave-shaped fin crest along its head and neck, the paddle tail edged with one wave pattern.
STAGE 3 (water/ice): a big heavy platypus beast on four thick legs like a guardian lion, a huge flat paddle tail, the crest grown into one big smooth pale-cyan ice crest down its head and back, a few large smooth ice plates on its shoulders.
Main colour sky blue with white; accent pale cyan ice / aqua only on the bubble, wave and ice.
