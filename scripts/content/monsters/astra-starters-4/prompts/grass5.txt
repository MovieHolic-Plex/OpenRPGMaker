LINE: GRASS starter line (stage 3 becomes grass/psychic). A sloth family that grows plants on its fur. Design thread: a little leaf cap on its head that becomes a blooming lotus, and long gentle arms with big curved claws.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby sloth hugging its own knees, a huge round cream face with dark eye patches around enormous round shining eyes and a sleepy happy smile, soft moss-green fur, a small round leaf cap like a beret on its head, short arms with three small cream claws.
STAGE 2: a chunky young sloth standing upright with long arms hanging down, its back covered with a thick mossy mantle and a few broad leaves, the leaf cap grown into a leafy bud, a calm smile.
STAGE 3 (grass/psychic): a big serene sloth sage sitting in a floating meditation pose, very long arms folded, a big mossy mantle with ferns, a large blooming pink-violet lotus flower crowning its head like a third-eye crown, peaceful half-closed eyes.
Main colour moss green with cream face; accent pink-violet only on the lotus.
