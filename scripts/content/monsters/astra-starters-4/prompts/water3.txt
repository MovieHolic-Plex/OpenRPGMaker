LINE: WATER starter line (stage 3 becomes water/ice). A platypus family. Design thread: a BIG broad flat pale-yellow duck bill, a wide paddle tail, and a water bubble that becomes an ice crest.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby platypus, a huge round head with enormous round bright eyes and a big clearly visible broad pale-yellow duck bill smiling, smooth sky-blue body with a white belly, four stubby webbed feet, a wide flat paddle tail with one big round water bubble on its tip.
STAGE 2: a sturdy young platypus standing on its hind legs, a bigger bill, webbed hands, a single curling wave-shaped fin crest on its head, the paddle tail edged with one wave pattern.
STAGE 3 (water/ice): a big strong platypus guardian standing upright with a broad white chest, a huge flat paddle tail, the head crest grown into one big smooth pale-cyan ice crest like a crown, two smooth ice shoulder plates. Few, large ice shapes, not many small spikes.
Main colour sky blue with white; accent pale cyan ice / aqua only on the bubble, wave and ice.
