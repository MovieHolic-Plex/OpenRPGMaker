LINE: FIRE starter line (stage 2 and 3 become fire/fighting). A ferret family. Design thread: a long body with a flame mohawk on its head and a ringed tail that becomes a fire whip.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby ferret, huge round head, enormous round bright eyes, small round ears, red-orange fur with a cream face mask and belly, a small crisp pixel flame tuft on top of its head like a candle flame, a short fluffy ringed tail, standing on four short legs.
STAGE 2: a slim fast young ferret standing up on its hind legs in a martial-arts stance, the head flame grown into a flame mohawk, cream wrist bands, a long ringed tail ending in a flame.
STAGE 3 (fire/fighting): a powerful upright ferret fighter, muscular arms with flaming fists, a big blazing flame mane running from its head down its back, a very long ringed tail lashing like a fire whip, heroic fierce but friendly face.
Main colour red-orange with cream; accent bright yellow-orange only on the flames. Flames are crisp flat pixel shapes with a dark outline, not glowing.
