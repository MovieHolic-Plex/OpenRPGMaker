LINE: FIRE starter line (stage 2 and 3 become fire/fighting). A ram family. Design thread: curly cream wool and curled horns that start as little embers and become blazing horns; a headbutting fighter.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby lamb on four short legs, a huge round head with a red-orange face and enormous round bright eyes, a big fluffy cloud of cream wool on its head and body, two tiny curled nub horns glowing ember orange, a small puff tail.
STAGE 2: a sturdy young ram standing on its hind legs in a boxing stance, red-orange body, a thick cream wool collar and wool wraps on its fists, two medium curled horns with small flames at their tips.
STAGE 3 (fire/fighting): a big powerful ram champion standing upright, a broad chest with a thick cream wool mane, muscular arms with dark hoof-fists, two huge spiral horns wreathed in crisp flat flames curling around its head, a brave friendly face.
Main colour red-orange with cream wool; accent bright yellow-orange only on the horn flames. Flames are a few big crisp flat pixel shapes with a dark outline, not glowing.
