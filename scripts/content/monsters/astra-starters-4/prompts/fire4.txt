LINE: FIRE starter line (stage 2 and 3 become fire/fighting). A boxing-kangaroo family. Design thread: tall rounded ears, big feet, and a thick tail whose tip is a flame; the fists turn into flame boxing gloves.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby kangaroo joey, a huge round head with a cream face and enormous round bright eyes, two tall rounded ears, warm orange-red body with a cream belly, tiny paws held up like fists, a short thick tail tipped with one small crisp flame, big feet.
STAGE 2: a lean young kangaroo boxer standing tall in a fighting stance, cream bandaged fists, a longer thick tail with a bigger flame tip, a confident grin.
STAGE 3 (fire/fighting): a big muscular champion kangaroo with a broad cream chest, two huge round fists that are solid flame boxing gloves, a thick strong tail with a big flame tip, heroic brave expression. No other flames on the body.
Main colour orange-red with cream; accent bright yellow-orange only on the flames. Flames are a few big crisp flat pixel shapes with a dark outline, not glowing.
