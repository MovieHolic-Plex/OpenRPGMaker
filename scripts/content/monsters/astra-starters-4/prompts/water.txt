LINE: WATER starter line (stage 3 becomes water/ice). A platypus family. Design thread: a broad flat duck bill, a wide paddle tail, and a water-droplet bubble that grows into ice armour.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby platypus, huge round head, enormous round bright eyes, a short rounded pale-yellow duck bill with a happy smile, smooth sky-blue fur with a white belly, four stubby webbed feet, a wide flat paddle tail carrying a big round water bubble balanced on its tip.
STAGE 2: a sturdy young platypus standing on its hind legs, fin-like webbed hands, a wave-shaped fin crest on its head, the paddle tail now edged with a curling water-wave pattern, a brave smile.
STAGE 3 (water/ice): a big strong platypus guardian standing upright, a broad body, clear pale-cyan ice crystal armour plates on its back and shoulders, a huge paddle tail rimmed with ice spikes, a wave crest on its head grown into an ice crown, calm protective eyes.
Main colour sky blue with white; accent pale cyan ice / aqua only on the bubble, waves and ice.
