LINE: GRASS starter line (stage 3 becomes grass/psychic). A leaf-lemur family. Design thread: two big round leaf ears, a long tail with green-and-cream rings that curls into a spiral, and a flower bud on the forehead that opens as it evolves.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby lemur sitting, an extra large round cream face with enormous round amber eyes, two big round leaf-shaped ears, a small closed pink bud on its forehead, a plump round leaf-green body, a long ringed tail curled into one big spiral beside it.
STAGE 2: a STURDY plump young lemur standing on its hind legs (thick body, not skinny), still a big round head and big eyes, bigger pointed leaf ears, the bud half open, the ringed tail curling high above its head in a spiral.
STAGE 3 (grass/psychic): a tall solid lemur sage standing upright with a short cape of a few big broad leaves, the forehead flower fully open in bright pink-violet with a glowing pink orb at its centre like a third eye, the huge ringed tail forming a big spiral circle behind it like a halo, a large friendly face.
Main colour leaf green with cream; accent pink-violet only on the flower. Keep shapes large and simple.
