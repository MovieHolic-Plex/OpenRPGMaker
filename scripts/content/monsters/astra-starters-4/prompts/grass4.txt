LINE: GRASS starter line (stage 3 becomes grass/psychic). A leaf-lemur family. Design thread: two big round leaf ears, a long tail with green-and-cream rings that curls into a spiral, and a flower bud on the forehead that opens as it evolves.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby lemur sitting, a huge round cream face with enormous round amber eyes, two big round leaf-shaped ears, a small closed pink bud on its forehead, a plump leaf-green body, a long ringed tail curled into one big spiral beside it.
STAGE 2: a young lemur standing on its hind legs, the leaf ears bigger and pointed, the bud half open, two broad leaves on its forearms, the ringed tail curling high above its head in a spiral.
STAGE 3 (grass/psychic): a tall calm lemur sage standing upright with a short cape of a few big broad leaves, the forehead flower fully open in pink-violet, the huge ringed tail forming a big spiral circle behind it like a halo, wise friendly eyes.
Main colour leaf green with cream; accent pink-violet only on the flower. Keep shapes large and simple.
