LINE: GRASS starter line (stage 3 becomes grass/psychic). A leaf-lemur family. Design thread: a long curling vine tail with leaf-green rings, and a closed flower bud on the forehead that opens as it evolves.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby lemur, huge round head, enormous round amber eyes, leaf-green fur with a cream face and belly, two small round leaf ears, a tiny closed pink flower bud on its forehead, a long curled vine tail with leaf rings, sitting up with tiny paws.
STAGE 2: a slim agile young lemur standing upright, longer limbs, broad leaf fronds growing from its forearms like sleeves, a long striped vine tail curling high above its head, the forehead bud half open.
STAGE 3 (grass/psychic): a tall serene forest-sage lemur standing upright, a long cape of layered leaves from its shoulders, the forehead flower fully bloomed like a glowing third eye (pink-violet accent), the vine tail forming a big spiral halo behind it, calm wise eyes.
Main colour leaf green with cream; accent pink-violet only on the flower.
