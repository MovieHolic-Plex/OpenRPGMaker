LINE: WATER starter line (stage 3 becomes water/ice). An orca (killer whale) family that walks on stubby flipper feet. Design thread: the black-and-white orca pattern with white eye patches, and a dorsal fin that grows into an iceberg.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby orca calf waddling on four tiny stubby flipper feet, a big round bean-shaped head-and-body, navy-blue top with a white belly and big white oval patches around enormous round shining eyes, a small rounded dorsal fin on its back, a little tail fluke, a happy smile.
STAGE 2: a sturdy young orca standing upright on two flipper feet, broad flipper arms, a taller curved dorsal fin edged with frost, a confident smile.
STAGE 3 (water/ice): a huge powerful orca guardian standing upright, a broad white chest, big strong flipper arms, the dorsal fin grown into a tall smooth pale-cyan iceberg crest, a heavy tail fluke, calm protective eyes.
Main colour deep navy blue with white; accent pale cyan ice only on the fin and iceberg.
