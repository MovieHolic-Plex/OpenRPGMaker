LINE: FIRE starter line (stage 2 and 3 become fire/fighting). A boxing-kangaroo family. Design thread: a thick tail whose tip is a flame, and paws/fists that glow with fire as it evolves.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby kangaroo joey, huge round head, enormous round bright eyes, big tall rounded ears, warm orange-red fur with a cream face and belly, tiny paws held up like little fists, a short plump tail tipped with a small crisp pixel flame, standing on big feet.
STAGE 2: a lean young kangaroo boxer standing tall on big feet, a fighter's stance with raised fists wrapped in cream bandages whose ends smoulder with small flames, a longer tail with a bigger flame tip, a confident grin.
STAGE 3 (fire/fighting): a big muscular champion kangaroo, broad chest, huge round fists burning like blazing flame boxing gloves, a flame mane on its neck and shoulders, a thick strong tail with a roaring flame tip, heroic brave expression.
Main colour orange-red with cream; accent bright yellow-orange only on the flames. Flames are crisp flat pixel shapes with a dark outline, not glowing.
