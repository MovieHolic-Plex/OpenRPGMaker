LINE: WATER starter line (stage 3 becomes water/ice). A baby walrus family. Design thread: two little tusks that grow into ice spears, and a water-spout fountain on its head.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby walrus pup, a huge round head, enormous round bright eyes, a round puffy white muzzle with two tiny rounded tusks, smooth sky-blue body with a pale belly, two front flippers, a small water fountain spout sprouting from the top of its head like a little geyser.
STAGE 2: a chunky young walrus sitting up proudly, longer tusks, a bigger water spout crest on its head, flippers with wave-shaped edges.
STAGE 3 (water/ice): a huge strong walrus guardian rearing up, two long tusks turned into crystal-clear ice spears, a frost-white beard, icy crystal armour on its shoulders, a tall frozen fountain crown on its head.
Main colour sky blue with white; accent pale cyan ice / aqua only on the spout and ice.
