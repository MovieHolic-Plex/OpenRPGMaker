LINE: GRASS starter line (stage 3 becomes grass/psychic). A pangolin family whose scales are leaves. Design thread: overlapping broad leaf scales like an artichoke, and a round curled tail.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby pangolin sitting up, a huge round cream face with enormous bright eyes and a tiny pink nose, its back and head covered by a few big overlapping fresh-green leaf scales like a little hood, a short round curled leaf tail, stubby paws held to its chest.
STAGE 2: a bigger young pangolin standing on its hind legs, rows of larger pointed leaf scales on its back and arms like armour, a long leaf-scale tail curling up behind it, a confident smile.
STAGE 3 (grass/psychic): a big noble pangolin sage standing upright, a great cape of huge leaf scales over its back and shoulders, a tall crest of leaves on its head holding one glowing pink-violet flower like a gem, a long heavy curled tail, calm wise eyes.
Main colour leaf green with cream face and belly; accent pink-violet only on the flower.
