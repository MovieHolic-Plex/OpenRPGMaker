LINE: FIRE starter line (stage 2 and 3 become fire/fighting). A ferret family. Design thread: a single flame tuft on the head that grows into a flame mohawk, and a long tail with red and cream rings ending in a flame.
STAGE 1: a tiny round baby ferret on four short legs, a huge round head with a big cream face mask and enormous round bright eyes, small round ears, red-orange body, one small crisp flame tuft on top of its head, a short fluffy ringed tail.
STAGE 2: a slim young ferret standing on its hind legs in a boxing stance, cream wrist wraps, the head flame grown into a short flame mohawk, a long ringed tail ending in a small flame.
STAGE 3 (fire/fighting): a strong upright ferret fighter with a broad chest and thick forearms, big dark fists, a tall flame mohawk running from forehead to the back of the neck (the only big flame on the body), a very long ringed tail ending in a flame, brave friendly face.
Main colour red-orange with cream; accent bright yellow-orange only on the flames. Flames are a few big crisp flat pixel shapes with a dark outline, not glowing, not many small flames.
