The original PNG is redistributed unchanged as opengameart-castle-tiles.png.
OPRN Studio uses 16px editing cells (32 columns) to preserve the original
32px artwork at its native size. One original tile occupies 2 x 2 cells.
Editor collision, layer defaults and labels are supplied by OPRN Studio.
