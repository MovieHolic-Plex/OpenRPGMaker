VERDICT: FAIL

Reviewer: fresh adversarial Visual QA (version 3, 2-cell vine rows). Images checked: v3_rows_1x/2x/4x, v3_tiles_4x, v3_cells_split_8x, beodeul_refs_4x, ref_tree-1a786c_4x.

MAJOR
1. [Rule 1: no top plane / front fold] The 2-cell block still reads as a single front-facing box hedge, not a canopy top over a front face. Top cells (all 8) and the upper half of the front cells use the same clump size, the same lime highlight and the same density. The "back-to-front gradient" only shows as a dark band in the bottom ~5 px of the front cell (8x split, lower row). At 1x and 2x the whole row is one uniform green rectangle with a purple hem. Nothing marks where the top plane ends and the front face begins: no brighter top band, no darker undercut where the canopy rolls over. Compare the Beodeul pergola (refs far left). Its flat, finely textured top plane sits over a clearly darker front edge. To fix: make the top cell clearly lighter and flatter (smaller clumps, top-lit, less crevice depth). Put a readable rolled lip at about 2/3 of the block height: a row of lit clump tops over a dark crevice line, with a mid tone below. The front face should step down by a full value, not just in its last few rows.
2. [Rule 4: square-cut ends] The left end and right end still cut off as a flat vertical foliage wall (4x rows: the left edge of every row is a straight vertical line from top to bottom). The 2-piece shortest row (bottom left of rows_4x) reads as a green box between two sticks. To fix: round the end silhouette in both top and front cells. Taper the top corners, let one or two clumps sag past the post, and give the front corner its own shadow clump.
3. [Rule 3 + Rule 4: top-plane grapes] Each top cell (left, m1..m6, right) has exactly one 3x3 grape near the same height. In rows they repeat at a regular 16 px spacing (clearly visible across the 4x and 2x rows). At 1x they are still dark purple specks or holes in the foliage. The glint pixel does not survive at 1x/2x. To fix: remove most top-plane grapes (grapes hang under leaves, so they should sit on the front face). Keep at most one in some middles, vary its x/y, and make it a lighter violet with a clear highlight.
4. [Rule 4: periodic grape hem] The front grapes form a ruler-straight fringe on the same baseline, 2 per cell at almost the same offsets in every middle (m1..m6 in the 8x split). At 1x/2x this reads as a purple tassel trim along the hem, the most periodic element in the row. To fix: vary bunch heights by 2-4 px. Hang some bunches higher, half-hidden under a leaf lip. Leave some cells with 1 bunch and some with 3. Use varied bunch sizes (3-5 px wide, 4-7 px tall) with a light-violet top-left pixel.

MINOR
5. Mid-post caps (m2, m5 top cells): the orange cap sits alone on the top edge with no post visible beneath it in the top cell. At 1x/2x it reads as a stray orange pixel speck on the hedge, not a post. Either show 1-2 px of post shaft going into the canopy with a shadow, or drop the cap.
6. End posts: flat 2-tone sticks, taller than the canopy and slightly detached from it. They have no lit top face (3/4 view) and no cast shadow. The grey wire stub sticking out from the end post at the bottom looks like a stray mark. Give the post a 1-px lit cap and a shadow, and run the wire visibly into the foliage or remove the stub.
7. Ground shadow: the dark brown checker-dither band under the row is an ordered checkerboard that shows as a mechanical stripe at 4x and a thin dark line at 1x. It sits directly under the row rather than offset to the lower right (light from upper left). Use a solid darker earth value with a soft irregular edge, extended 1-2 px further on the right side.
8. Palette and contrast vs Beodeul trees: the vine highlights are a brighter, more saturated lime than the Beodeul trees. The darkest crevices are not as deep as the tree's near-black blue-green undersides, so the clumps look soft. The rows look like a generic bush tile rather than Beodeul foliage. Pull the highlight toward the tree's yellow-olive, and deepen the underside and crevice tone on the front face.
9. Trunks under the canopy are short, evenly spaced brown stubs that repeat with the cell rhythm. Acceptable, but vary spacing and lean more. Some cells should show no trunk at all.

WHAT IMPROVED (not enough to pass)
- The top silhouette is now lumpy and the stacked two-tier balls / straight crevice from v2 are gone.
- No hard seams between middles were visible. Brace strokes are removed. Clump overhang across the cell boundary works.

Summary: v3 fixes the v2 construction defects, but it still fails the core 3/4 read (no top plane vs front face). The ends are still square, and the top-grape and hem-grape placements create a visible 16 px periodic rhythm. FAIL.
