# RPG Demo Quality Bar

Use this checklist before calling an editor-authored demo "a game."

## Minimum Passing Demo

- Goal is visible in the first minute.
- Player has at least one optional interaction.
- Player must move through a purposeful map space.
- At least one object or NPC changes after an action.
- A battle or challenge has a reason to exist.
- Reward changes future state.
- The player returns to someone or somewhere changed.

## Score Bands

Under 20:

- Mostly a technical proof.
- Talk/fight/stop structure.
- No meaningful state change.

20-40:

- Has a quest idea but weak map/reward/aftermath.
- Player can complete something, but the world barely reacts.

40-60:

- Has a complete loop.
- Needs better pacing, clearer rewards, and richer interactions.

60-80:

- Feels like a small RPG slice.
- Has map purpose, choices, rewards, and aftermath.

80+:

- Has multiple connected loops, readable progression, polish, and replayable authoring templates.

## Current Target

Move the demo from under 20 to 50+ by adding:

- quest state,
- interactable object,
- reward,
- post-completion dialogue,
- map gate,
- and playtest inspection evidence.
