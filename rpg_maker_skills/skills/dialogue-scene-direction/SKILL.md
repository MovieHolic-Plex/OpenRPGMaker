# Dialogue Scene Direction

Dialogue should direct action.

## Line Roles

Hook line:

- Names the problem.

Objective line:

- Tells the player what to do.

Hint line:

- Points to a landmark or mechanic.

Reaction line:

- Changes after completion.

Reward line:

- Confirms what changed.

## Rules

- Keep one message window to 1-3 short lines when possible.
- Use speaker names consistently.
- Pair choices with a real consequence or clear information.
- Change dialogue after switches/variables change.
- Avoid lore dumps before the player has acted.

## Scene Shape

Good first quest dialogue:

1. Problem.
2. Request.
3. Direction.
4. Confirmation after player accepts.
5. Different closure after completion.
