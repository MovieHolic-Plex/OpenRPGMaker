# Small House 01 Layer Grid

Coordinates are zero-based from the top-left of the 16 x 15 template.

`-1` means no upper-layer tile. Lower-layer grass/base is tile `240`.

## Corrected Layer Rule

Current rule for this template:

- Fence is lower transparent overlay.
- Windows are lower transparent overlay.
- Door is lower layer.
- House wall, straight roof body, and roof-to-wall boundary are lower layer.
- Slanted roof overlays are the only upper layer tiles.

The historical source pattern had fence and windows in the upper array. The editor now normalizes those ids into `lowerTileStacks`; do not recreate them as upper tiles.
