Most scripts require a `.env.local` with `VITE_SUPABASE_URL`, `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`, and `VITE_LLM_API_KEY` (or `VITE_VITE_LLM_API_URL`/`VITE_VITE_LLM_MODEL`). Run with `npx tsx scripts/<script>.mts` or `node scripts/<script>.mjs`. The dev supervisor is started via `scripts/_start-dev-hard.ps1` (Windows) or `node scripts/dev-keepalive.mjs --detach` (Unix), which spawns a long-lived process watching Vite on `RPGZZU_DEV_PORT` (default 9999).
