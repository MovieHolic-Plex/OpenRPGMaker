# 백발 여도사 — original native pixel candidate

## Recovery and source

The existing 18-color palette and nine finished base poses were retained. This
continuation authored the missing nine action grids. No other candidate, game
store, ledger, brief or repository content was read or edited. The three supplied
reference images were observed for adult proportions, restrained faces, layered
cloth and readable grips; no image pixels were copied into the source.

All deliverable grids contain 64 literal ASCII rows of 64 symbols. `author.py`
is the retained base-pose provenance; `actions_author.py` records explicitly
chosen row runs for the nine new actions. Helpers only write those runs and
decode the grids for inspection. No shape masks, generated shading, pose
transforms, interpolation or generated imagery were used.

## Identity and materials

- Rounded silver-white bun with a small jade pin, two long rear hair ribbons,
  compact adult front cheek and warm, sparse wrinkle marks. Both eyes look right;
  eyelids are independently closed for sleep. Stun uses an actual open mouth.
- Quiet round sleeves, pale layered long coat, deep teal crossing collar and
  belt, unequal split hems and separate charcoal shoes. The standing idle extends
  from y8 to y60, a native height of 53 pixels.
- Hair uses pale H/L planes and M/S shadow groups; broad cloth planes use L/M
  with B in deep folds. Warm F/E/R skin remains separate from the cool robe.
  The staff has a lit V edge and U wooden shadow. Folded paper uses P/H/W.
  Light is from upper left. Dark O/D clusters describe deep separations rather
  than a black border around every patch.

## Explicit new choices

- `skill_a`: talisman lifts in front of the chest; left forearm crosses the body,
  right elbow raises the staff grip, small jade clusters gather at paper and tip.
- `skill_b`: bent knees and open forward elbow, uneven protective lotus mantle
  curving up around the abdomen and chest. Open petal tips rise past the head;
  the face stays visible. Paper and staff cores stay connected to their hands.
- `skill_c`: lowered palm, unfolding sleeve and restored offset stance; torn
  upper petal fragments and a final pale edge on the descending talisman.
- Poison: abdomen cradled in the first pose; a deeper bent-knee cough and hand
  brought to the mouth in the second. Narrowed lids, pinched warm mouth and two
  different arrangements of hollow jade toxic bubbles.
- Stun: rearward slack head and dropped wrists, then bowed head and wider
  buckled knees. Low dangling paper and a loose, angled staff grip. Two separately
  placed cream star arrangements; no text or generated glyphs.
- Sleep: closed short eyelids, lowered staff, chin tucked toward the collar;
  a deeper nod, fuller upper sleeve and vertically folded rear hair on exhale.
  The paper remains in the small connected hand.
- New staff grips were inspected and repaired with explicit short rows so the
  skin fingers wrap the wooden shaft without an unintended sideways jump.

The compact face and tiny folded paper are deliberately restrained and may be
hard to read at native size on a pale battlefield. The soft jade protection
outline likewise has less contrast on pale cream than on dark terrain. The
fallen pose and its horizontal staff are retained from the interrupted draft.
Animation cadence, effects in a real battle and an independent aesthetic review
remain for the parent harness. No user choice or approval is claimed.
