### Comparative Analysis
*   **P10 vs P12 (Quay Steps):** P10 is **PASS**. It correctly implements the reference's 2px light/2px dark stair tread pattern. P12 is **FAIL**; its shading is inconsistent and muddy, failing to integrate with the existing canal edges.
*   **P11 vs P13 (Canal Lock):** P11 is **PASS**. It uses the proper Beodeul-style timber/stone texture. P13 is **FAIL**; its gate is too vibrant/saturated, making it stand out awkwardly against the muted palette of the reference canal.

### Overall Assessment
Round 2 analysis revealed several pieces (P3, P9, P12, P13) failing to adhere to the strict 3/4 perspective or the established Beodeul palette. Pieces P3 and P9 specifically failed on stylistic grounds (flat heraldry fill, flattened perspective). P10 and P11 are recommended for inclusion, while P12 and P13 remain rejected as they do not meet the visual integration standard required next to reference assets.
