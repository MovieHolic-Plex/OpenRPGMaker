# Visual QA Review: 16px Pixel-Art Chipset Pieces

### Review Summary
The candidate pieces were evaluated against the Beodeul chipset reference material for:
1. **3/4 Top-Down Perspective:** Horizontal surfaces (tops, decks) are visible; front-facing surfaces (south) are visible; no side or pure front-elevation views.
2. **Style Match:** Palette, light source (upper-left), and outline density match the Beodeul reference.
3. **Readability:** Objects are recognizable at 1x scale.
4. **Specific Conformity:** Canal edges, stairs, and water features align with the established Beodeul canal and weir aesthetics.
