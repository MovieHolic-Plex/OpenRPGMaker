c09 observations (round pink-stone tower with conical roof)

A. 3/4 view: The cone roof is seen from above with tile courses and a clear upper surface that narrows to a finial, the same read as the Beodeul conical tower reference. The shaft has a curved front with stone string courses that curve slightly. Only the south face is shown and there are no isometric side faces. The stone base platform shows its top and front edge.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom. No band of grass or background shows through the tower. The roof eave sits directly on the shaft with no gap. The base platform and step meet the ground at the bottom edge. There is exactly one door (the arched studded timber door). The windows (dark pane, lit yellow pane, narrow slits) are readable, and the ones near the left and right edges are foreshortened as expected on a cylinder. Nothing is floating or cut off. A tiny yellow lantern dot beside the door reads as a small light and is not a second door.

C. Style: The stone has varied block sizes, light and dark tones, and dark purple-toned joints, comparable to the Beodeul tower. The roof tiles vary across several orange and brown tones. Outlines are dark and tonal. The palette is harmonious with the references. Minor points: two isolated blue pixels sit on the right edge of the string courses at about x 205, y 425 and y 550 on the 4x board, the windows are fewer and plainer than the Beodeul reference, and the joint colour is a little saturated. None of these are blocking.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
