c05 review (1x and 4x boards)

A. 3/4 view: The round tower shows a curved front with banding rings, and the conical roof has a visible eave ring and finial. The cone is seen with a slight overhang, matching the Beodeul round tower reference. Only the south face is visible, with no side faces. The stone base platform is a walkable slab seen from above with a step at the front.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no horizontal or vertical grass band through the tower. The roof, shaft and base are continuous. The tower stands on a plinth, and the bottom edge meets the ground. There is exactly one door, an arched wooden door at the base. The windows are small dark-glass openings, not holes to the background. Nothing floats or is cut off.
C. Style: The roof tiles use several orange and brown steps with row texture, similar to the reference cone. The stone blocks have varied sizes and shading, and the outline is a dark mauve, not black. The pinkish-mauve stone is somewhat more saturated than the reference's cream plaster, and the mortar lines are a little heavy and regular. The windows are smaller and sparser than the reference's. These are minor deviations, and the tower still harmonises with the reference palette and reads as the same game.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
