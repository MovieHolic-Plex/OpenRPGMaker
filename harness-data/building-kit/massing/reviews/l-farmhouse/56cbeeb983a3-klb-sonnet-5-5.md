c01 - half-timbered cottage with red tile roof and gabled cross-wing

A. 3/4 view: Main roof shows a broad upper surface (several tile rows), comparable to the Beodeul cottage reference. Only the south facade is visible; the cross-wing gable reads as part of the same building, no ground between parts, no isometric side face.
B. Structure: No holes or floating parts. Walls and wing both sit on a stone sill that meets the ground. One door (plank door in the wing). Chimney is attached to the roof. Nothing garbled.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in tone with highlights and moss specks, plaster has stipple, timber frame in dark brown tones, outline is a dark material tone rather than black. Colours harmonise with the Beodeul references. Minor: the gable peak pole is a little sharp, but not blocking.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
