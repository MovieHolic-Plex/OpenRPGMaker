c07 (courthouse-style half-timber house: hip roof with two chimneys, timber upper floor, stone ground floor, one arched door with a scales sign)

A. 3/4 view: The roof upper surface is large and reads as a real surface with hip slopes at both ends, matching the Beodeul inn and tavern reference. Only the south facade is visible. No isometric side faces.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands through the roof, the timber band or the stone base. The two chimneys stand on the roof and overlap it, so they do not float. The eave meets the timber wall with no gap. The bottom edge meets the ground, apart from a few grass tufts at the base corners that are normal. There is exactly one door (the arched door with the scales sign beside it). The other openings are windows.
C. Style: The roof tiles vary in tone with dark wear patches, the plaster is stippled, the timber frame has diagonal braces, and the stone base is a pink and purple cobble like the reference. Outlines use dark brown tones, not black. The palette is nearly identical to the Beodeul references. The roof is a little darker and more blotchy than the reference, which is a mild difference, not a blocking one.
