The clock tower sits on the roof ridge with a cone roof seen from above. Only the south facade is shown, with no isometric side wall.
B. Structure: No grass or background bands through the building at 4x. The roof sits directly on the wall and the tower base sits on the roof. The bottom edge meets the ground along the full width. There is exactly one door (arched plank door in the centre arcade bay). The two side arcade bays are dark stone recesses with a bench and a notice board, not doors. The banners and clock are attached to the wall and tower. The few green specks on the roof edge and at the base are moss details, not gaps.
