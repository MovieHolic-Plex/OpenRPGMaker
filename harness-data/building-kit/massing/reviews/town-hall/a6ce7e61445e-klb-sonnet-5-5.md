c06 (town hall: hip roof, clock turret with spire, upper wall with banners, ground-floor arcade, one door)
A: Hip roof with a real shaded upper surface and a top plateau. The clock turret sits on the ridge with a conical spire seen from above. Only the south facade is shown. The arcade reads as a front face; no side faces.
B: The building is one solid mass, with the bottom edge on the ground. One central arched door; the side arches hold a window and a notice board, not doors. No holes or grass bands at 4x. The turret base meets the roof top without a gap.
C: Roof tiles are shaded in several steps and match Beodeul. The cream stone facade has coursing, shutters, banners and lit windows, with dark brown outlines. The cream fills are large and the arcade infill is a bit flat, but it is textured and harmonises.
MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
