c06 (town hall: blue tiled hip roof, clock tower with conical roof, stone upper wall, ground-floor arcade with one arched door)

A. 3/4 view: The main roof shows a large upper surface with hip slopes at both ends, the same treatment as the Beodeul hip roofs. The clock tower sits on the roof ridge and reads as part of the same building, with its own small conical roof seen from above. Only the south facade is visible. The arcade is a front elevation. No isometric side faces.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands through the roof, tower, stone wall or arcade. The tower base overlaps the roof, so it does not float. The eave meets the wall with no gap. The bottom edge meets the ground along the whole width. There is exactly one door (the arched wooden door in the middle bay). The two side bays are open alcoves with a bench and a notice board, not doors. The few green dots on the roof edge and at the bottom corners are moss and grass tufts, not background gaps.
C. Style: The roof tiles have several tones with highlight edges and the stone courses vary in tone, with pale pillars and dark inner arcade stone. Outlines use dark material tones, not black. The blue roof is a different hue from the terracotta Beodeul houses, but it matches the blue church roof in the reference set, so it harmonises. The three arcade bays are somewhat similar, but each has different contents, so this is not a stamp repetition.

VERDICT: PASS
