c07 - wide stone-and-plaster hall with two-tier red tile roof and centre gabled porch

A. 3/4 view: Roof is a real surface, but it is split into two tiers by a wooden band with four small dormer windows, which reads as a stacked/double roof rather than one coherent roof volume. South facade only.
B. Structure: One door under the porch. The porch base appears to extend slightly below the wall base line, so the bottom edge does not align with the ground line of the main wall. Nothing floating otherwise.
C. Style: Six identical narrow windows in a row and uniform tile rows give visible stamp repetition. Colours are acceptable but the surface is flatter than the Beodeul references.
