c01 review (1x and 4x boards)

A. 3/4 view: The roof top surface is a large, real tiled plane, about a third of the facade height, comparable to the Beodeul reference hip roofs. Only the south facade is visible. The hip sides on the left and right are roof planes, not wall faces. The two dormers sit on the roof surface and read as roof features. There is no isometric side wall.
B. Structure: Scanning the 4x board top to bottom, I found no horizontal or vertical band of grass or background through the building. The bottom edge meets the ground with a gray base outline. There is exactly one door, an arched wooden door at bottom centre-left. The chimney sits on the roof at the right and is joined to it. Nothing is floating or cut off.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in several orange, red and purple steps, with a highlighted left hip edge, and look consistent with the references. The plaster stipple, stone quoins, string courses and shuttered windows with sills and flower boxes closely match the references. Outlines use dark brown or gray, not black ink. The chimney is gray stone where the references use brick, a minor difference. The lit yellow window is a small flat fill, acceptable at this size. The two green moss specks on the roof are minor. The look is the same game as the references.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
