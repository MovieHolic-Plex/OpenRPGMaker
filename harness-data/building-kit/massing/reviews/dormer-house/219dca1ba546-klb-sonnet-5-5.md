c06 observations (three-storey plaster house with two dormers)

A. 3/4 view: The terracotta roof has a large visible upper surface with tile courses and hip slopes at both ends. Only the south facade is shown and there are no isometric side walls. The roof reads as a real surface.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom and found no band of grass or background through the building. The bottom edge meets the ground, and there is exactly one door (the arched timber door). However, the left dormer is garbled. Above its small gable there is a tall dark purple and orange vertical block (about x 90-175, y 250-335 on the 4x board) that does not match the right dormer, which has a clean small roof. This block does not read as a dormer roof, a chimney or a hip. It looks like a corrupted or misplaced shape, and it is not an intended detail. There are also isolated green pixels on the roof (about x 165, y 262 and x 415, y 370) that read as stray marks.

C. Style: The plaster has stipple and the corner quoins and string courses give stone detail. The roof tiles vary in tone. Outlines are dark and tonal. The palette matches the Beodeul references. The grey stone chimney differs from the reference brick chimneys, and the window grid repeats. These are minor.

MAJOR: The left dormer roof is a garbled dark and orange block that does not read as any real roof form, and it is inconsistent with the right dormer.
VERDICT: FAIL
