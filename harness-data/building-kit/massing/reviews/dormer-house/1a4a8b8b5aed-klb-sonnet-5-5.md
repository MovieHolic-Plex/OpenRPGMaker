c04 (red-tile hip-roof cottage with brick chimney, barrels, sacks)

A. 3/4 view: The roof upper surface is a large, real hip roof with a lit left hip, a dark right hip and a flat ridge top, comparable in depth to the Beodeul cottage. Only the south facade is visible and there are no isometric side walls. Chimney stands on the roof surface.
B. Structural integrity: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom and found no horizontal or vertical band of grass or background through the building; the roof is continuous from ridge to eave and the eave sits directly on the wall. No holes, no floating parts. Exactly one door (arched, framed in stone). The base meets the ground. Barrels and sacks are separate props at the foot of the wall, attached to the ground line.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in tone row by row, plaster is stippled, the base has stone courses, outlines are dark brown/purple tones and not black. Colours (terracotta roof, cream plaster, purple-pink stone base) harmonise with the Beodeul cottage next to it. Minor points: the red brick chimney is slightly taller and redder than the grey Beodeul chimney, and the roof speckle on the upper left is a little noisy. These are not blocking.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
