c08 (small gable-front cottage: plaster and timber walls, stone chimney, gable window, one arched door, flower-box window)

A. 3/4 view: The roof shows a lit orange slope and a shadowed dark red slope above the gable triangle. This is the same two-tone gabled treatment the Beodeul gable cottage uses, and the roof reads as a real surface and not a thin strip. Only the south facade is visible. No isometric side faces.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands through the roof, the gable, the timber beam or the wall. The chimney overlaps the roof slope, so it does not float. The eave and gable trim meet the wall with no gap. The bottom edge meets the ground. There is exactly one door (the arched plank door at left). The other opening is a window with shutters and a flower box.
C. Style: The roof tiles are shaded in several steps with bright and dark courses, the plaster is stippled, the timber frame has a darker brown outline, and the chimney stone has varied tones. Outlines use dark material tones, not black. The orange lit-side roof is a little brighter and more brick-like than the reference, a mild harmony issue only. Proportions and palette match the Beodeul cottage next to it.
