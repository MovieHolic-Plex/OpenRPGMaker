c02 (half-timbered house with a round stone tower and orange cone roof)

A. 3/4 view: Pass. The main roof shows a large upper surface with real tile rows, not a thin strip. Only the south facade is visible. The left roof end is cut diagonally like a hip, with a bright edge highlight. It leans slightly toward a side view but still reads as a hip end, not a side wall. The tower shows a curved front with a cone seen from above, and a stone band ring. House and tower read as one building.
B. Structure: Pass. I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands or slits. The roof and the tower cone meet with a dark diagonal join and no ground shows between them. The bottom edge meets the ground. There is exactly one door (arched wooden door on the house). The tower has windows only. The chimney sits on the roof ridge and is not floating.
C. Style: Pass, with notes. The roof tiles vary in tone, the plaster has stipple, and the timber frame is in browns. Outlines are dark plum or brown, not black. The lower wall's dark purple mortar grid is heavier and more regular than the Beodeul stone, so it reads a little like a grid stamp. The tower brick is similar. The window flower boxes are repeated, but the repetition is acceptable. The warm orange roof is in the same family as the Beodeul house roofs. It looks like it belongs in the same game.

VERDICT: PASS
