A. 3/4 view: The main roof shows a large upper surface of varied clay tiles, with a hip-end facet on each side matching the reference roof construction. The tower shows a curved front and a cone seen from above. Only the south facade (timber framing, windows, door) is visible. The tower and house read as one building with no ground between them.
B. Structure: No transparent holes or floating parts. Exactly one door (arched wooden, in the stone base). The stone plinth under the tower and the house wall both meet the ground. The chimney is attached to the roof. Tower windows are small and a little loose on the wall but readable.
C. Style: Multi-step shading on the roof, plaster and stone. Outlines are in material-dark tones, not black. Plaster stipple, timber framing and stone courses are present. Colours harmonise with the Beodeul references. Roof tile rows are fairly regular (mild stamp feel) and there are small green moss patches on the roof that the references lack, but these are minor.
