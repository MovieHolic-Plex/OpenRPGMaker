c10 (half-timber house over a stone ground floor, hip roof with chimney, attached round stone tower with conical roof, one arched door)

A. 3/4 view: The main roof upper surface is large and reads as a real surface, with a hip slope at the left end and a shadowed hip edge at the right where it meets the tower. The conical tower roof is seen from above with lit and shadowed courses, the same treatment as the Beodeul tower roofs. Only the south facade is visible. The tower overlaps the house corner and reads as one building. No isometric side faces.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands through the roof, the timber band, the plaster, the stone base or the tower. The chimney overlaps the roof back slope, so it does not float. The tower roof overlaps the main roof edge with no gap. The eave meets the wall with no gap. The bottom edge meets the ground along the house and the tower. There is exactly one door (the arched plank door in the stone base). The other openings are windows, and the hay or sack pile by the door is a prop, not a door.
C. Style: The roof tiles are shaded in several steps with dark wear patches, the plaster is stippled, the timber frame is well outlined, and the stone base and tower use varied pink and purple cobble courses with a stone door arch. Outlines use dark material tones, not black. The palette and proportions match the Beodeul references. The roof is a little blotchy and the tower stone slightly more purple than the reference, a mild harmony issue only.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
