c03 review (1x + 4x boards)

A. View: The hipped roof shows a real upper surface with a right-hand hip. The cylindrical tower shows a curved stone front with a cone roof seen from above. Only the south facade is visible. The tower roof overlaps the main roof's hip, so the two parts read as one building. This passes.

B. Structure: No holes. There is one door, the arched door on the stone base. The house bottom edge meets the ground. The tower's bottom is rounded and sits slightly lower than the house baseline, with its right edge dissolving at the bottom. It has no plinth or base course, unlike the Beodeul tower, which has a grey stone footing. The tower windows are tiny dark slits at uneven heights and read as stray marks. The tower body appears to start in front of the house wall with its left edge cutting into the plaster. That join is acceptable but crude.

C. Style: The house roof tiles, plaster stipple and half-timber match the Beodeul inn. The tower is weak. Its stone is very pale, low contrast and noisy, and its right third turns into purple dither speckle. The dark outline fades out along the right edge and bottom. The Beodeul tower keeps a clean, consistent outline and readable tonal bands. The tower is also much paler than the warm house, so it looks like a different asset. The chimney is a narrow grey block that differs from the brick reference. The tower cone roof is slightly more saturated and orange than the main roof.

MAJOR:
- Tower right edge and bottom lose the dark material outline and dissolve into dither noise, unlike the Beodeul outline discipline.
- Tower stone reads as speckle, not courses, and does not harmonise with the house.
- Tower has no base or plinth and does not stand on the ground like the references.

VERDICT: FAIL
