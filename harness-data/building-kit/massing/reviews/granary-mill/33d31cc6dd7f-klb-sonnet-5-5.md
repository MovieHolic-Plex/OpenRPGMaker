c05 - half-timbered house with stone round tower and hoist rope (4x board 940x542)

A. 3/4 view: The house hip roof shows a real upper surface. The tower is a curved stone front with a tiled cone seen from above, and it overlaps the right hip of the house roof. The tower and house are joined without ground between them.
B. Structure: At the join to the left of the tower (about x=440-455, y=300-370 in the 4x board) there is a lighter rectangular patch of a different wall tone that does not belong to either the plaster or the tower stone. It reads as a seam or a pasted block. A rope with a sack hangs from the beam in the middle of the facade and ends in mid-air, a floating element. One door (arched, lower middle). Two windows in the tower sit side by side with a third below, which reads as a face.
C. Style: Roof tiles, stone courses, plaster stipple and outlines match Beodeul well. The tower cone is lighter and more orange than the house roof, which is consistent with the Beodeul tower. Green flecks are acceptable.

MAJOR: rectangular off-tone patch at the house-tower junction reads as a pasted seam and breaks the single-building read.
MAJOR: the rope and sack dangle in mid-air from the beam, a floating, unreadable element.
