c01 - wide manor with cross-gable wings and a gabled porch, purple slate roof

A (3/4 view): The main roof shows a large upper surface and the south facade only, so the view reads correctly. The two end gables and the porch gable are attached to the main body. The recessed centre wall stops higher than the wings and porch, which leaves grass between the three bottom edges. That reads as a courtyard notch, not as one footprint. This is acceptable but weak.
B (integrity): One door in the porch. No holes. The roof silhouette at the far left and right ends is boxy and stepped, and the hip ends look cut off against the gable roofs. A dark vertical shadow strip on the left of the centre wall reads as a leftover, not as intended shading.
C (style): The roof is saturated violet-blue. It does not harmonise with the terracotta Beodeul roofs beside it at 1x and looks like a different game. The tiles form a regular brick grid, not the scale-tile variation of the references. Window shutters and flower boxes are repeated stamps in a regular pattern. The stone ground floor is good. The outline is mostly dark brown, which is correct.

MAJOR:
- Roof colour and tile pattern (saturated purple brick grid) do not match the Beodeul references at the same scale.
- Boxy, cut-off looking hip ends at the left and right of the roof, plus a stray dark vertical strip on the centre wall.
- Courtyard notch leaves ground between the wings and the porch, so the wings do not read as one footprint with the main body.

VERDICT: FAIL
