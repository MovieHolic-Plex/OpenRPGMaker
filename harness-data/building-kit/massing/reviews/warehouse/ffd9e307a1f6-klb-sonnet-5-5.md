c06 - narrow three-storey red brick townhouse with a terracotta tile roof, a white-coped stepped gable, shuttered windows on each floor and one arched wooden door.

A. 3/4 view: The terracotta roof is a large, visible, textured upper surface, which is good, and no side faces are shown. The gable geometry is inconsistent, though. The white stepped coping runs diagonally across the roof, with brick wall inside the steps and roof tiles both outside and above the steps. A real stepped gable would put the roof behind the steps, not on both sides and above them. The roof therefore reads as a backdrop behind a stepped wall, close to a flat front elevation, and the brick triangle inside the steps looks pasted on.

B. Structure: No grass or background band cuts through the building at 4x. The building meets the ground with a thin grey base. Exactly one door (the arched door at the bottom). The centre window of the top floor shows a pale blob that is hard to read (a face or a sack), and the two centre openings below it look like a crate and a shuttered hatch rather than windows, so the facade is partly unreadable. A stray green tuft sits on the roof at the upper left, and there are pale vertical edge strips on the roof flanks.

C. Style: The tile colour and the brick red are in the Beodeul family, and the outline is a dark material tone. The brick is a large, nearly flat red field with regular rows and very little block shading compared with the Beodeul pink brick. The window rows are identical stamps repeated on every floor (same shutter, same sill, same spacing). The grey floor bands are flat single-colour lines. The building is narrower and plainer than the references and would stand out as a different drawing style.

MAJOR:
- A: stepped gable and roof geometry is contradictory (roof tiles outside and above the steps), so the roof does not read as one coherent pitched surface.
- B: unreadable centre openings (pale blob in the top window, crate/hatch-like openings below) and stray tuft/edge strips on the roof.
- C: large flat brick field and identical stamped window rows do not match the Beodeul shading.

VERDICT: FAIL
