c02 (timber-frame shop with market arcade, dormer)

A. 3/4 view: Wide terracotta roof with a clearly visible upper surface, and narrow hip faces at both ends (lit on the left, shaded on the right). This is the same construction as the Beodeul inn reference, so it is not an isometric side wall. Only the south facade is shown. The dormer sits on the roof surface and reads as part of it.
B. Structure: No grass or background showing through at 4x. The bottom edge meets the ground along its full width. One door (right, arched plank). The arcade bays hold stalls and barrels, which is readable and not garbled. The small stone block with a lantern beside the door is a little ambiguous but is attached to the wall and not floating.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in several tones with a lit ridge edge, and the half-timber wall has stipple, matching the references closely. Outline is a dark brown, not black. The stone arcade is a bit lighter and cleaner than the Beodeul cobble base, and the grey dormer cap is slightly off-palette, but both are minor. It would sit comfortably next to the references.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
