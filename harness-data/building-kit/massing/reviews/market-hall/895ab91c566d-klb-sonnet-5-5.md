c01 (market hall: tiled hip roof, half-timber upper floor, stone arcade stalls below, small dormer)

A. 3/4 view: The roof reads as a large real upper surface with a hip end on each side, same treatment as the Beodeul reference. Only the south facade is shown. The dormer sits on the ridge and reads as a small roof feature seen from above. The ground-floor arcade is a front elevation, which is normal for this view. No isometric side faces.
B. Structure: At 4x I scanned top to bottom and found no grass slits or cuts through the roof, timber band or arcade. The bottom edge meets the ground. There is exactly one door (the arched wooden door at right). The small wall lantern and window next to it are not doors. Nothing is floating or garbled.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in several steps with highlight and shadow tones, the plaster is stippled, timber frames and stone pillars are shaded, and outlines use dark material tones rather than black. The palette matches the Beodeul references (same terracotta roof, cream plaster, brown timber). Moss specks on the roof add texture. Some repetition in the window shutters is acceptable.
