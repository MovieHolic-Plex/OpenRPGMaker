c02 - stone smithy with open forge lean-to (4x board 876x414)

A. 3/4 view: The main hip roof is fine and reads as a surface. The right-hand forge wing is the problem. Its roof is a steep, narrow slab whose top edge climbs to the left, with a distinct dark vertical seam against the main building. It reads as a side-facing sloped plane or a separate cut-out, not as part of the same building seen from the south. The wing does not read as ONE mass with the house.
B. Structure: The wing roof's right end is chopped off with a stepped diagonal and looks cut. The wing front is an open dark interior, so the mass looks incomplete. The main house has one door, and the wing has none, so the door count is fine. The bottom edge meets the ground.
C. Style: The main house stone courses, shutters, anvil sign and chimney are good and close to the Beodeul smithy. The wing roof is darker and more orange than the main roof, so the colour jumps at the seam. Green flecks repeat on the roof.

MAJOR: the wing roof reads as a side face or a separate steep slab with a dark seam, so the building is not one 3/4 mass.
MAJOR: the wing roof is cut off at its right end and the open dark front looks unfinished.
