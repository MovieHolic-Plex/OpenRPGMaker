c08 observations (stone windmill on a flagstone platform)

A. 3/4 view: The round tower is drawn with a curved front and a domed brown shingle roof whose upper surface is visible, the same read as the Beodeul round-tower reference. Only the south face is shown and there are no isometric side faces. The flagstone platform shows a top surface with a front edge. The four sails sit in front of the dome on a central hub, which is a normal windmill form.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom. No band of grass or background shows through the tower, the dome, or the platform. The dome sits directly on the tower with no gap. The sails are lattice frames attached at the hub, so the open space between their bars is by design and not a hole. The platform meets the ground along its bottom edge. There is exactly one door (the arched studded timber door). The small window under the hub is readable. The sack pile beside the door is readable. Nothing is garbled or cut off.

C. Style: The stone has varied block sizes, light and dark tones, and dark joints, and it is comparable to the Beodeul tower. The shingles on the dome vary in tone with dark rows. Outlines are dark and tonal. The palette matches the references. Minor points: a slightly pinkish-purple outline tint shows on the tower sides, the sail lattice is thin and fairly uniform, and the platform slab is a little regular. None of these are blocking.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
