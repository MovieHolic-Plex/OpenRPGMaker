c02 (round windmill tower with thatch dome, four sails, stone base, arched door)

A. 3/4 view: The tower body and dome follow the Beodeul round-tower pattern, but the four sails are drawn as flat front-elevation panels laid over the tower and the dome. They have no depth, no foreshortening and no top surface, so they read as a flat elevation pasted onto a 3/4 building. The vertical upper sail cuts straight across the dome face and hides the thatch cap. The dome shows only its front, with no readable top surface.
B. Structural integrity: No grass slit or hole seen on the 4x board. One door, the base meets the ground. The sail hub looks like it floats on the tower front with no support, and the upper sail overlapping the dome reads as garbled/overlapping parts.
C. Style: Thatch cap has some texture. The sail slats are large, almost flat single-colour cream/orange blocks with no multi-step shading, which is clearly weaker than the Beodeul reference. The plaster body is smooth with little stipple and few stone courses compared with the reference tower. The sails' brown outline is close to black ink compared with the material-tone outlines of the reference.

MAJOR:
- Sails are flat front-elevation sprites (no 3/4 depth), the upper sail slices through the dome.
- Large flat single-colour fills on the sails; the body lacks the textured stone/plaster courses of the Beodeul tower.

VERDICT: FAIL
