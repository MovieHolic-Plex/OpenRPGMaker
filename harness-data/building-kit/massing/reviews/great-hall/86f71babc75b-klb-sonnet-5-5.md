c04 (stone chapel with a grey hipped slate roof, chimney and buttressed south facade)

A. 3/4 view: The hipped roof is a large visible upper surface. It has sloped front and side hip planes and a ridge flat on top, so it is clearly not a thin strip. Only the south facade is shown, with buttress pilasters and Gothic windows. There are no isometric side faces. The side hips taper correctly toward the ridge and read as roof planes seen from above. The chimney sits on the roof surface and shows its top.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board from top to bottom. There are no grass bands or slits through the roof or the wall. The eave overhang meets the wall top cleanly. The bottom edge sits on the ground with a dark plinth. There is exactly one door, centred under a small stair or step. Nothing floats. The windows are readable, and one stained-glass window with red marks is slightly busy but not garbled.

C. Style: The roof has scale texture in several grey steps with a few moss spots, and the outline is in the roof's dark tone, not black. The wall has varied stone courses, pale pink accent blocks and a lit/shadow side to each buttress. The grey roof differs in hue from the Beodeul blue-purple roofs, but it harmonises with the grey castle stone in the same game. The roof is large and its texture is a little uniform, and it lacks a ridge highlight line. These are minor and do not look out of place next to the references.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
