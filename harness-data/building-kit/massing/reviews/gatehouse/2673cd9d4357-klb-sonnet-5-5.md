c01 gatehouse with two round towers

A. 3/4 view: The rear rampart deck is visible as a real walkable surface (pink stone slab with a rear crenellated parapet and a front crenellated lip), not a thin strip. The two towers show a curved cylindrical front plus a conical blue tile roof seen from above, with a finial and a pennant. Only the south facade is shown; no isometric side faces. The wall and towers read as one building.

B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no horizontal or vertical grass band through the building. The grass beside the upper rear wall is outside the silhouette, not a cut. The towers meet the wall continuously. The base sits on the ground with a light grass fringe. There is exactly one door (arched portcullis gate with a wooden door). The hatch and the gold emblem on the deck are small details and read fine. Nothing is garbled.
