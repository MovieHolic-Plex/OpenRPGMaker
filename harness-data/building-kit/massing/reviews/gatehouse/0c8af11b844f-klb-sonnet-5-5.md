# c03 (twin-tower gatehouse)

A. 3/4 view: The two cylindrical towers show a curved front and a cone roof, with a crenellated band under each cone, and this works well. The curtain wall between the towers shows a parapet and walkable top, but at 1x that top is only about 3px, so it reads close to a flat front elevation. The Beodeul tower reference shows a much larger walkable top surface. Only the south facade is visible, with no isometric sides.

B. Structure: The gate archway shows a flat, single-colour green fill straight through the building. It is the same colour as the background grass, so it reads as a transparent hole. There is no passage floor or depth, and the portcullis and arch frame sit against it. The building has two entrances: the big gate arch and a separate small arched wooden door at the base of the left tower. That breaks the "exactly one door" requirement. The walls and stairs meet the ground, and the towers read as part of one building.

C. Style: The stone shading, outlines and purple cone tiles match the Beodeul castle well, and the banners and crest are fine. The cone roofs have a chunky, repeated tile stamp but are acceptable.

MAJOR:
- The gate arch is a transparent or background-coloured hole, with no interior or passage floor.
- There are two entrances (the gate arch and the left tower door), so it does not have exactly one door.
- The walkable top of the curtain-wall parapet is only about 3px at 1x, too thin to read as a 3/4 surface.

VERDICT: FAIL
