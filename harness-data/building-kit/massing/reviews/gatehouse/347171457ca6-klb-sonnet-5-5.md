c05 - castle gatehouse: two round towers with blue conical roofs, a crenellated front wall with a large arched wooden portcullis door, banners, and a pink-brick wall-top walkway behind the front merlons.

A. 3/4 view: The wall-top walkway is a real visible surface (pink brick courses, a chest and a gold emblem on it) bounded by a front merlon row and a rear parapet, so the top-down read works. Both towers show a curved front, a corbel band and a cone roof seen from above. Only the south facade is visible; there are no isometric side faces. Towers and wall read as one building.

B. Structure: Inspected the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass or background band through the wall, the towers or the gatehouse. The towers join the wall directly with no gap. The bottom edge meets the ground. Exactly one door (the central arched gate). The small dark crosses and the small window on the towers are slits, not holes. Nothing is garbled or floating.

C. Style: Close to the Beodeul castle. The blue shingle cones use the same blue and the same multi-step shading as the shipped roofs. The stone has several steps of grey and pink-tinted blocks, with a dark purple-grey outline in the material tone and not black. The wooden door has banding and rivets. Block shapes repeat somewhat but no more than the Beodeul stone. It would sit beside the shipped castle without standing out.

MAJOR: none
