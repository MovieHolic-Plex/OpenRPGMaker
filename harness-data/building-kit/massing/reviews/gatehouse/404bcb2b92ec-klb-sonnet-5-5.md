c01 (gatehouse, two round towers with blue cone roofs, curtain wall between)

A. 3/4 view: Pass. The wall between the towers shows a walkable top surface with a crenellated back edge and a front parapet band, so it is not a thin strip. Only the south facade is visible. The towers show a curved front with a cone seen from above, and a dark parapet ring below the cone. No isometric side faces. Towers and wall read as one building.
B. Structure: Pass. I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands, slits or cuts through the building. The base meets the ground. There is exactly one door (an arched portcullis gate with a wooden door). The small brown plaque and gold gem on the walkway surface are odd and slightly unclear, but they do not read as holes or floating parts.
C. Style: Pass, with minor notes. The stone has several shading steps, varied courses and pink/grey stipple, the cone roof tiles vary, and the outlines are in dark stone and blue tones rather than black. The palette matches the Beodeul castle (same blue roofs, same grey stone). The two banners are near-identical stamps, and the walkway floor is a bit flat. Both are minor.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
