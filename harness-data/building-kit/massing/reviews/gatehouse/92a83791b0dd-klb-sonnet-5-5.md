c05 - castle gatehouse: two round towers with conical blue roofs, crenellated curtain wall, central gate

A (3/4 view): The towers show a curved front and a cone seen from above. The curtain wall shows its crenellated walkable top. Only the south facade is visible. The towers and the wall read as one building with no ground between the parts.
B (integrity): One gate with a portcullis arch; no second door. No transparent holes. The bottom edge meets the ground. Banners and slits are readable.
C (style): Stone coursing and the pale brick band have several shading steps. The blue conical roofs match the blue Beodeul castle roofs. Slits are dark rather than pure ink in most places. Minor risks: the pale brick band is somewhat flat, and the black cross slits are on the dark side. Neither is blocking.
