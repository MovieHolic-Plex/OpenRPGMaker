c04 observations (blue-roofed stone hall)

A. 3/4 view: The roof has a large visible upper surface with tile courses, and the left and right hip slopes are shown with a brown edge line. Only the south facade is visible and there are no isometric side walls. The three stone chimneys sit on the roof surface. The view matches the Beodeul blue-roof reference.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom and found no band of grass through the building. The roof overhang meets the wall course without a gap. The stone corner quoins run the full height. The bottom edge meets the ground. There is exactly one door (the arched door at the centre). Nothing is garbled. Windows, banners and lit panes are readable.

C. Style: The grey stone walls have varied block sizes, pink-toned accent blocks, a mid-height string course and quoins, so the shading has several steps. Outlines are dark and tonal. The blue roof tiles vary and the palette matches the Beodeul castle and tower references. Weaknesses: the window row repeats evenly and the roof is a little dark. Both are minor and not blocking.
