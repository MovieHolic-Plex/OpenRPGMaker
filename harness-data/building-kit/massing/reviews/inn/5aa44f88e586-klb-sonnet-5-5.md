c02 review (1x + 4x boards)

A. View: The main hipped roof shows a full upper surface with a right-hand hip. The lower wing roof also shows an upper surface with its own hip. Only the south facade is visible, with no side faces. The wing joins the main roof's hip with no ground between them. This passes.

B. Structure: No holes or floating parts. The bottom edge meets the ground along the whole width, with a thin base strip under the stalls too. There is one door, the arched plank door on the stone base. The wing's two open stall bays are market openings, not doors. The roof, wall, base and wing are all connected. Nothing is garbled. The small wall lantern next to the door is readable.

C. Style: Roof tiles vary in tone, matching the Beodeul inn. The plaster stipple and half-timber frame match too. The pink-purple stone base has courses like the reference. The outlines are dark brown or plum, not black. Window frames and shutters are shaded in several steps. The two grey chimneys use a cool grey stone texture that differs from the reference's brick chimney. They are narrow, but they read as chimneys and are a minor mismatch. The wing's stall interiors (grain pile, bottle rack) are small and a bit dark, but they stay legible at 1x. The overall palette harmonises with the references.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
