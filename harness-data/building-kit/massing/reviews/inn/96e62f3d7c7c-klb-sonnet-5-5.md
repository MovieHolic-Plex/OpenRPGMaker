c06 review

A. 3/4 view: A half-timbered shop with a lean-to market bay on the right. The main roof is a hip roof with a large upper tile surface, a lit left facet and a dark right facet, the same construction as the Beodeul reference. The lean-to has its own roof surface with a dark end facet. Only the south facade is visible (timber-framed upper wall with four windows, stone ground floor, arched door, hanging sign, open stall with goods). Two brick chimneys stand on the roof ridge. No isometric side walls. The lean-to reads as attached to the main house.
B. Structure: No transparent gaps or holes. Exactly one door (arched, studded wood, left). The open stall on the right is a counter bay with shelves and goods, not a second door. The stone base and the wooden stall base both reach the bottom edge, so the building stands on the ground. The chimneys sit on the roof. Nothing floats or is cut off. The lean-to roof starts right where the main roof ends, with no gap between them.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in tone with highlights on the ridge and lit facet. The plaster has stipple, the timber framing is clear, and the stone base has varied courses with mortar. Outlines are in material-dark tones, not black. Window boxes and the sign match the Beodeul reference. Colours harmonise with the references. Minor: roof tile rows are fairly regular, and there are a few small green moss specks on the roof that the references lack. None of this is blocking.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
