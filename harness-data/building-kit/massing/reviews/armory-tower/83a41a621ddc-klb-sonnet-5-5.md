c04 square stone tower with rampart deck

A. 3/4 view: The top deck is visible as a real walkable surface (cream brick floor, about 25px tall at 1x) with a rear crenellated parapet, side parapet strips, and a front lip of white merlons. There is exactly one door (arched plank door with iron studs). The three arrow slits are small and scattered, and read as slits. Nothing is garbled.
