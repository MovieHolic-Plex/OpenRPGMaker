c06 (square stone tower with an open furnished rooftop and one arched door)

A. 3/4 view: Mostly OK on the top, doubtful on the body. The rooftop is a real walkable surface with a parapet and front crenellation band, so the upper surface is visible. But the roof platform is clearly wider than the tower body below it. The body is inset on both sides, so the building reads as a narrow box under a wide lid. The right part of the body is darkened in a vertical band (about the last 15px at 4x), which reads as a shaded side face and not as part of a single south facade. That is the sideways perspective the brief forbids.
B. Structure: No gaps or cuts through the building. The grass beside the narrow body below the overhanging platform is background beside the building, not a hole. The base meets the ground and there is exactly one door (arched wooden door). The table, chest, barrel and small crate on the rooftop are readable but make the top look like a cluttered terrace, not a battlement; I treat this as a style concern and not a structural defect.
C. Style: Weaker than the Beodeul tower. The stone has large pure-white block patches and bright specks that look like stamped highlights, and the left side is much lighter than the right in a way that does not match the evenly lit Beodeul tower. The outlines are mostly dark grey, which is acceptable. The rooftop furniture is flat and small and not in the Beodeul tower's style. The windows are tiny slits scattered at uneven heights.

MAJOR: Dark vertical band on the right side of the tower body reads as an isometric side face (breaks the south-facade-only rule); the body is also much narrower than the rooftop lid so the parts do not read as one solid mass.
VERDICT: FAIL
