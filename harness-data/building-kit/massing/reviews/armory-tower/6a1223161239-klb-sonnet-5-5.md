c03 (square watch tower with a walkable rooftop platform)

A. 3/4 view: The rooftop is a real, wide floor surface. It shows a back crenellated parapet, side parapets and a front lip with a machicolation band. Props (table, chest, barrel, grate) sit on the walkable top. Only the south facade is visible. There are no side faces. The darker right-hand column of the shaft reads as shading on the front face, not a second face. The top reads as a surface, not a thin strip, which matches how the Beodeul tower shows its parapet top.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom. There are no grass slits or gaps through the tower. The platform overhangs the shaft slightly on both sides, and the grass beside the shaft is outside the building, as with the reference. The bottom edge meets the ground with a dark plinth. There is exactly one door, at the base. The three arrow slits are small and readable. Nothing is floating or cut off.

C. Style: The stone has varied course sizes and several shading steps, with a dark grey-blue outline in the material tone, not black. The colours match the grey Beodeul stone. The stone is slightly noisier than the reference, and a few pale pink blocks at the lower left look a bit random. The platform floor is a little large and plain, though it is broken up by the props. The shaft is a touch narrow for the platform. These are minor and do not look out of place in the same game.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
