The thin grey strip under the eave is a dark eave or gutter line, not background. The porch roof joins the wall with no ground between. The bottom edge meets the ground, and the porch foot sits slightly lower, which suits a projecting porch. There is exactly one door (the arched door in the porch). The chimney sits on the roof back slope. Nothing is garbled.
