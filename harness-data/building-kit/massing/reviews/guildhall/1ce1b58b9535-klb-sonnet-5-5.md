c06 - stepped-gable stone townhouse with purple tile roof and key sign (4x board 1196x862)

A. 3/4 view: The purple tiled hip roof shows a real upper surface behind the stepped gable (a trapezoid with visible tile rows and shading on the sides). The roof is only moderately deep, but it is a surface, not a strip. Only the south facade is visible and there are no side faces. The stepped gable and finial read as one front mass.
B. Structure: No transparent gaps found. The wall rises continuously to the roof, and the bottom edge sits on a grey plinth that meets the ground. Exactly one door (arched, lower left). The key sign is attached to the wall. The round attic window and the small gable windows are legible.
C. Style: Stone courses vary in several steps, outlines are in the dark stone tone, and the roof tiles vary. The purple harmonises with the Beodeul church roof and the pink-grey stone matches its walls. Window openings repeat with the same shape, but the Beodeul church repeats windows in the same way, so this is not a stamp-copy defect. No large flat fills.
