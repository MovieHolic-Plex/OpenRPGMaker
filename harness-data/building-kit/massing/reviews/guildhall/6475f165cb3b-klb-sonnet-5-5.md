c04 review

A. 3/4 view: A stepped (crow-step) gable guildhall. The purple slate roof is visible behind and beside the stepped gable as a real tiled surface, with the stone step caps showing their top faces. Only the south facade is visible: three storeys of windows, a round window, an arched door and a key sign. There are no isometric side faces and no sideways perspective. It reads as one tall building.
B. Structure: No transparent gaps or holes. Exactly one door (arched, studded, bottom centre). The stone plinth along the bottom meets the ground across the full width. Nothing floats or is cut off. The roof ends at the left and right edges with straight vertical cuts and no eave or overhang, which is a bit blunt but not broken.
C. Style: The stone wall has the same pinkish brick-stone courses with mortar as the Beodeul church, plus horizontal string courses, dark-toned window frames and a light-grey stone plinth. The slate roof has tile variation and highlights. Colours match the references (same purple roof family, same wall palette). Minor weaknesses: the roof edges lack the dark eave line the references have, and the windows are evenly spaced, which looks a little stamped. Neither is blocking.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
