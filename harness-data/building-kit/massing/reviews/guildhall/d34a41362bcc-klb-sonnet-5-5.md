The grey area behind the steps is a vertical wall with faint brick lines and a vertical light/dark split near the centre. It is not a roof surface seen from above. The brief requires the roof's upper surface to be visible as a real surface. Here there is no ridge, no tile slope and no top surface, only a front face with a stepped outline. Compared with the Beodeul references, whose roofs are large visible tile planes, it reads as a flat front elevation.
