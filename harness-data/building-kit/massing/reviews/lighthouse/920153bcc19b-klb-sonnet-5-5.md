c02 observations (lighthouse)

A. 3/4 view: The gallery ring shows its walkable top as an ellipse, seen from above. The lantern room sits on it under a copper cone roof, which is also seen from above. The tower body is a cylinder with a curved front and tapered silhouette, like the Beodeul round tower. No side faces. The base is a rock outcrop, and the steps are on the south side only.

B. Structure: I inspected the 4x board top to bottom and found no horizontal or vertical band of background through the building. Gallery, lantern, roof and shaft all join without gaps. The bottom edge sits on rocks that meet the ground. There is exactly one door at the base. The small window and lit lantern are readable. Nothing is floating or cut off.

C. Style: The red and white bands carry brick and stone courses, with several tones per material. Outlines are dark in the material's tone and not black. Roof tiles on the copper cap vary. The palette matches the references, though the red bands are a little flat and darker than the references. This is minor and not blocking.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
