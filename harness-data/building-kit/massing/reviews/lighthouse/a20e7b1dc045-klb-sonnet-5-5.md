c03 review (1x and 4x boards)

A. 3/4 view: The cylindrical shaft shows a curved front with a taper. The gallery ring at the top is an elliptical walkable surface with a railing lip, seen from above. The lantern room sits on it and has a conical copper roof with a finial, also seen from above. Only the front is shown, with no side faces. It matches the "curved front plus cone/parapet" requirement.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no horizontal or vertical grass band through the tower. The lantern, gallery, red and white bands, and base are all continuous. The tower stands on a rock base with steps, and the bottom meets the ground. There is exactly one door, an arched wooden door at the base. The one small window is a window, not a hole. Nothing floats or is cut off.
C. Style: The stone and brick bands are textured with several shading steps, and the copper roof has tile rows. The outline is a muted mauve or pink tone, not black, and is readable. The glowing lantern is a somewhat flat yellow fill, but small. The red and white striping is more saturated than the Beodeul tower reference, but it fits the subject and the palette is harmonious. The rocks have light moss accents. It plausibly belongs in the same game.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
