c02 review (1x and 4x boards)

A. 3/4 view: The top is a real walkable terrace surface, with a crenellated parapet on the back and sides and a front parapet lip. A small roofed pavilion with a barrel and a crate stands on the terrace. The tower body shows only the south face, with no side faces. The top plane is proportionally comparable to the Beodeul tower reference. It reads as one tower.
B. Structure: I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no horizontal or vertical grass band through the building. The corbel and machicolation band joins the body without a gap. The base meets the ground. There is exactly one door, an arched wooden door with a gold cross at the bottom. The arrow slits are slits drawn in the stone, not holes. Nothing floats or is cut off.
C. Style: The stone courses use several grey steps with varied block sizes. The outline is dark grey, not black. The red tile roof on the pavilion matches the references' palette. The look is close to the Beodeul tower reference, with the same stone, slit, and corbel vocabulary. Minor points: the bottom edge has a small grass tuft, and the terrace floor is a little flat. Neither blocks acceptance.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
