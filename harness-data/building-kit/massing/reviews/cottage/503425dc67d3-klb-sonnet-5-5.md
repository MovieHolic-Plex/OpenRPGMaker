c03 (small timber-frame house)

A. 3/4 view: The roof is a continuous hip roof with a visible upper surface, a lit left slope and a dark right slope. It has the same form as the Beodeul reference, and the chimney sits on the ridge. Only the south facade is shown, with no extra side faces beyond the reference's own hip ends. The roof front slope is a real surface, not a thin strip.
B. Structure: The roof is continuous with no transparent gap, unlike c02. Wall and roof read as one body. There is exactly one door and one window. The bottom edge meets the ground. Nothing is cut off or floating.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in several steps with moss specks. Outlines are in dark brown and not black. Diagonal timber bracing is fine, and the plaster has light speckle. Colours harmonise with the references at 1x and 4x. Minor: the plaster areas are slightly flatter than the reference, and the roof tile pattern is a bit regular. Neither is blocking.
