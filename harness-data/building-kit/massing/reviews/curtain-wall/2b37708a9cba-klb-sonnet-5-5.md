c04 (crenellated curtain wall with one round gate tower, slate cone roof)

A. 3/4 view: The wall shows a rear merlon row, a walkable rampart floor (with barrels and crates on it), a front merlon row, and then the south face. The walkable top is therefore visible as a surface. The tower is a cylinder with a curved front face and a cone roof seen from above, with no isometric side face. Only the south facade is shown. The tower and wall read as one structure with no ground between them.
B. Structure: No horizontal or vertical bands of grass or background showing through at 4x. The wall and the tower base both meet the ground. There is exactly one door (arched plank door at the base of the tower). The small crates, barrels and a hatch on the rampart are attached to the walkway and not floating. The tower slit windows are dark slits and not holes.
C. Style: Stone courses vary in length and tone and the outline is a dark grey, not black ink. The merlons are regularly spaced, which is natural for a battlement. The lower half of the wall is a fairly large light-grey expanse with sparse course lines, so it is plainer than the Beodeul castle wall. The cone roof is a dark slate with little tile detail, a weaker match than the blue tile roofs of the reference but still within the same palette family. It is acceptable as a plain curtain wall at this scale.

MAJOR: none
