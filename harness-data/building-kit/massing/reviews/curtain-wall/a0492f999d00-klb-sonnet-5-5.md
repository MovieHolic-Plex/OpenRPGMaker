c04 - long crenellated curtain wall with one round tower (grey cone roof) at the right and an arched door at the tower base. Barrels, crates and a bone pile sit on the wall top.

A. 3/4 view: The tower has a curved front and a cone roof seen from above, which is correct. The wall top is the weak part. The walkable top reads as only a thin one-or-two-pixel-row strip behind a row of merlons, and the wall face below it is a tall flat elevation. This is close to a flat front elevation with a token top. The tower roof is a small cone that is narrower than the tower body, so the tower looks like a pencil stuck on the wall. Props on the wall top (barrels, crates) appear to sit on a strip too thin to hold them.

B. Structure: No grass band or slit seen in the 4x board. The wall and tower meet the bottom edge. Exactly one door (tower base). The tower is a separate round shaft pasted over the wall with no corbel or junction, and the wall merlon row stops abruptly at the tower sides. The wall plus tower read as joined, not as a clean single building. The single door is at the tower only, so the long wall has no entrance, which is acceptable for a wall.

C. Style: Weak against Beodeul. The wall is a large light-grey area with faint, regular brick lines and almost no step shading; the Beodeul castle stonework has distinct block shading, darker cool shadows and a contiguous dark outline. The cone roof is a flat dark grey-green fill with only slight tile lines, which clashes with the saturated blue Beodeul roofs. The outline is a faint mid-grey, not a dark material tone. Merlon blocks repeat identically (stamp repetition). It would look washed out next to the shipped castle.

MAJOR:
- A: wall-top walkable surface is a thin strip; the wall reads as a flat front elevation.
- C: large flat low-contrast stone fills and a flat grey cone roof do not match the Beodeul shading or colours.

VERDICT: FAIL
