c01 (stone church, hip roof + front gable porch + round bell tower on the right)
A: Main roof is a real hip roof with a textured upper surface and a visible ridge band. The front gable porch reads as a cross-gable seen from the south. The round tower shows a curved front and a conical roof seen from above. Only the south facade is visible; no side faces.
B: Tower base and main wall share the ground line; roof and tower join with no grass band between parts. No holes or cuts at 4x. Exactly one door (the gable porch). Gable porch bottom sits on the ground.
C: Roof tiles are shaded in several steps with a brown edge, and they match the Beodeul tile family. The stone is cream coursed blocks with moss specks and brown/grey outline; it is paler and flatter than the purple-brick reference, but it is textured and harmonises. Minor: wall fills are fairly large and light.
MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
