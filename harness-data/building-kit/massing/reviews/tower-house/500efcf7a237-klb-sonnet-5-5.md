c04 (two-storey half-timbered house with a round pink-stone tower and orange cone roof)

A. 3/4 view: Pass. The main roof shows a broad tile surface from above with a hip end on the left, not a thin strip. Only the south facade is visible. The tower has a curved front with a cone seen from above and a dark rim ring under the cone. The tower overlaps the house's right edge and they read as one building.
B. Structure: Pass. I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands or slits. The grass visible above the right hip of the roof, next to the tower cone, is open background above the roof line, not a cut through the building. The tower and house walls meet with no ground between them. The base meets the ground along the whole width. There is exactly one door (arched wooden door on the house). The tower has windows only. The chimney sits on the roof.
C. Style: Pass, with notes. The roof tiles vary, the plaster has stipple, and the timber is in browns with diagonal braces. Outlines are in plum/brown tones, not black. The tower's dark purple mortar grid is heavy and regular and reads slightly like a grid stamp. The house has fewer windows and less detail than the Beodeul manor, but the palette and finish match.

MAJOR: none
VERDICT: PASS
