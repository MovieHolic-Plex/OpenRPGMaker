c05 review

A. 3/4 view: A half-timbered house with a stone round tower on the right. The hip roof shows a large upper tile surface with a lit left facet and a dark right facet, the same construction as the Beodeul reference. The tower shows a curved stone front and a cone roof seen from above. Only the south facade is visible. The tower and house read as one building, with no ground between them.
B. Structure: No transparent gaps or holes. Exactly one door (arched, studded wood, left). The house sill and the tower plinth both meet the ground. The chimney sits on the roof. Nothing floats or is cut off. The tower slit windows are small but readable.
C. Style: Roof tiles vary in tone and have highlights. The plaster has stipple and the timber framing is clear. The tower stone has courses and mortar. Outlines are in material-dark tones, not black. Colours harmonise with the references. Minor: roof tile rows are quite regular, there are a few small moss specks, and the facade has fewer windows than the reference. None of this is blocking.

MAJOR: none

VERDICT: PASS
