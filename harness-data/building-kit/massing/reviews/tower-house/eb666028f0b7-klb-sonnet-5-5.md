c01 review (1x + 4x boards)

A. View: The hipped roof shows a real upper surface with tile rows and a right-hand hip. Only the south facade is visible. The tower shows a curved stone front and a cone roof seen from above. The tower overlaps the roof's right hip with no ground between the parts. This passes.

B. Structure: No holes or floating parts. There is one door, on the house. The house bottom edge meets the ground along a thin gravel strip. The tower has no plinth or base course. Its bottom edge is cut flat and looks pasted onto the house baseline instead of standing on the ground. The tower windows are tiny slits. Two of them sit at uneven heights with no sill or frame, so they read as stray dark marks.

C. Style: The roof tiles, plaster stipple and half-timber are close to the Beodeul references. The tower is the weak part. Its right third dissolves into purple and pink dither noise, and its dark material outline fades out on the right edge. The Beodeul tower keeps a clean dark outline all the way round. The stone courses on the tower break up into random speckle instead of readable courses. The tower is pale and low contrast beside the warm house, so it looks like a different asset. The grey chimney is a flat, narrow block with a texture that differs from the roof and walls.

MAJOR:
- Tower right edge has no consistent dark outline. It dissolves into dither noise and does not match the Beodeul outline discipline.
- Tower stone reads as speckle noise, not stone courses, and its palette does not harmonise with the house.
- Tower has no base or plinth. It looks pasted onto the facade baseline.

VERDICT: FAIL
