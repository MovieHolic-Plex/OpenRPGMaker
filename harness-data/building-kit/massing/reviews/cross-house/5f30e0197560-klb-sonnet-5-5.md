c05 (single-storey half-timbered cottage with a central gabled porch and two chimneys)

A. 3/4 view: Pass. The main hip roof shows a large tile surface from above with hip ends on both sides. The porch gable pitches back into the main roof as a narrowing surface, in the same way as the Beodeul cottage at 1x. Only the south facade is visible. There are no side faces, and house and porch read as one building.
B. Structure: Pass. I scanned the 4x board top to bottom and found no grass bands or slits. The roof, wall and porch join without gaps, and the base sits on the ground. There is exactly one door (arched wooden door in the porch). Both chimneys sit on the roof slopes. The porch is slightly narrow, and a faint darker patch on the wall to the right of the porch roof looks like a soft shadow. It is a minor artifact and does not read as a hole.
C. Style: Pass. The roof tiles vary in tone, the plaster has stipple, the timber frame is in browns, and the porch gable has a lit and a shaded slope. Outlines are in dark brown or plum, not black, and the palette and window style match the Beodeul cottage. Window shapes are slightly repeated but are varied by flower boxes and sills. It looks like it belongs in the same game.
