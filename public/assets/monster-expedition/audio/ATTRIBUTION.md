# Original Starlight Island music and sound

Original composition and procedural sound design authored for OPRN Studio's
별빛섬 몬스터 원정 campaign, 2026-10-03, with the Codex coding assistant.

To the extent any rights exist, these original musical scores, generated audio,
and composition notes are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

There are no third-party audio samples, soundfonts, arrangements, recordings, or
quoted melodies. In particular, no Pokémon soundtrack melody is copied.
Python/numpy implement oscillators and envelopes; FFmpeg/libvorbis encodes the
result. Those tools are not distributed as campaign media.

Editable score: `scripts/content/monster-expedition-music.py`.
Generated asset inventory and hashes: `manifest.json`.
Arrangement and player integration: `COMPOSITION.md`.
