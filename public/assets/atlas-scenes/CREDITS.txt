The vehicle and scene pieces (ships, boats, airship, carts, festival and sky pieces) are painted by
scripts/content/atlas-scenes/vehicle_art.py, ship_parts.py, rig_art.py and scene_art.py. Their hull, deck, rail and
prop textures are sampled from the EasyRPG RTP chipsets, and some 16px cells are exact or flipped copies of upstream
cells. An attribution check on 2026-10-22 found 163 cells containing upstream pixels:

  ChipSet/Ship.png      88 cells
  ChipSet/Exterior.png  42 cells
  ChipSet/Dungeon.png   18 cells
  ChipSet/Interior.png   7 cells

Upstream: EasyRPG RTP, https://github.com/EasyRPG/RTP (pinned commit 993d88cbc78c658d348bbfa74a3b424d393d27e5).
Those four sheets are by JasonPerry (https://finalbossblues.itch.io/), released as CC0 within the RTP. The RTP as a whole
is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; full credit table: ../easyrpg/AUTHORS.md,
licence text: ../easyrpg/COPYING.
Changes were made: cells were cut, flipped, recoloured and re-composited with new hand-pixel parts.
This project's own pixels follow the OPRN default-asset terms (openwiki/licensing.md).
The canonical attribution is public/assets/ATTRIBUTION.md («EasyRPG RTP material in the Beodeul sheets and the vehicle sheet»).
