## Why I over-scored85
- I scored repairs relative to the old poor sprite and presence of cap/limb/bag features too generously, rather than calibrating completed native game craft against actual reference sprites.
- I gave silhouette16/anatomy16/walking17/pixelCraft13/identity14 despite box torso, sparse skin-column lower body, one-row eyes and flat color bands; these visible defects conflict with the frozen finished-art anchors.
- Hashes and two review records ensured traceability, not aesthetic truth; same-model root/critic organization did not provide decorrelated quality judgment.

The repair sequence satisfied feature-presence checks, but I treated those checks as evidence of finished craft. Actual reference comparison shows the remaining defects were material, not nonblocking polish. The old approval remains preserved as a historical record and should not be reused to claim the rejected artwork is excellent.

## Required redesign
- Rebuild proportions from measured compact dressed silhouettes: fuller head/cheeks, shorter exposed shin region and connected shorts/trouser/shoe clusters.
- Draw eye/face/hair/hat overlap as deliberate contrasting clusters rather than isolated dot eyes on a flat wedge.
- Redesign entire front/side torso, hips and steps as overlapping cloth/limb masses with planted contact and compact swing, not bare-column rearrangements.
- Reduce redundant navy colors, use meaningful dark contours/value ramps, and sculpt cap/vest/pack volumes as a coherent original design.
