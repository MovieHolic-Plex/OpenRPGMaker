# Harry Potter world concept v2

Producer: built-in image_gen. Purpose: visual direction proposal, not native game tiles or a completed map. v1 was rejected by the operator because it used a cinematic camera. No user approval is implied.

Create an OVERHEAD ORTHOGRAPHIC GAME LEVEL CONCEPT SHEET for a Harry Potter 2D top-down RPG. Three small compact separate room/zone cutaway dioramas side by side, seen at precisely the SAME steep overhead camera angle: look down about 65 degrees. This is NOT a film still. NO HORIZON, NO VANISHING POINT, NO RECEDING ALLEY, NO SKY. The floor's X and Y grid axes run exactly horizontal and vertical on the page, like an orthogonal Zelda A Link to the Past map, NEVER diamond/isometric. Roofs removed, north-facing rear wall interiors visible in shallow height; see the broad TOPS of all tables, the TOPS of all heads and shoulders, the full TOP of carriage roofs and horses' backs. View should read as a video game MAP FIRST. Pixel art style with bold clean shapes and tidy pixel clusters, restrained weathered stone/dark oak/aged brass/slate blue/moss green with amber lamps and small violet teal accents. Panel A: compact Hogwarts potions classroom, 4 distinct worktables with cauldrons, a teacher bench, pointed gothic back windows, shelves, robes, coherent stone floor. Panel B: compact Forbidden Forest carriage loading clearing, two different dark gothic enclosed horse carriages with large visible roof surfaces, two recognizably skeletal winged thestrals harnessed in front, small boarding platform, tree canopies at edges, clear compact path, robed scale figures. Panel C: compact snowy Hogsmeade owl post court, walls and roofless partial shop cutaways, owl perches and post sacks, warm windows and winter robes, snowy cobbles. Each panel fills its small zone densely yet legibly. Consistent human scale. Neutral dark canvas margins between the rectangular plans. No text, lettering, signs, labels or UI at all. No cinematic realism. No low camera. No tall architecture dominating. No empty expanses. A useful orthographic preproduction art board showing a coherent dedicated asset family.
